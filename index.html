Following Jesus shapes how we live and how we love. At Real Life Waller, we grow as disciples by putting God’s Word into practice and helping others follow Him.

Through everyday relationships, prayer, and time in Scripture, we learn to love our neighbors and serve together.

LEAD PASTOR

James Andrew

After 20 years in law enforcement, James retired early to enter full-time ministry. He completed his residency at Real Life Ministries Texas in Tomball and has a heart for relationships and making disciples.

James and his wife, Denise, married in 1998 and have a son, Austin. He enjoys fishing, shooting, and time with his family.

A PLACE TO BELONG

Grow. Connect. Serve.

View current events

Lifer Kids

Learning about Jesus should be a joyful experience. Through play, songs, Bible stories, and memorization, children learn about Christ’s love and what it means to follow Him.

Lifer Students

A ministry for students in grades 6–12. Connect with our church family and learn more about student gatherings through Church Center.

Young Adults

Real Life Waller also has a Young Adults ministry. Contact the church for current gathering details and ways to connect.

Men’s Ministry

Men build relationships and grow as disciples, husbands, fathers, friends, and leaders as they follow Jesus together.

Women of Waller

Women of Waller (WOW) gathers on the second Saturday of each month for connection and breakfast. Check Church Center for the next gathering.

Community Outreach

Our church shares the Gospel and serves alongside Waller Pregnancy Center and WARM. We seek to serve Christ in our personal lives, homes, and neighborhoods.
